About / Academic foundation

Curiosity, translated
into design questions.

I am an Arizona State University student specializing in electrical systems concepts, embedded control, and electromechanical integration. This portfolio presents my independent projects, Inhaler and Aetherion, with a focus on sensor feedback, control logic, actuator interfaces, and power management.

My project studies explore how electrical architecture and coordinated actuation support deployable mechanisms. Through configuration views, system diagrams, and technical explanations, each case study communicates the design approach and the relationship between electrical control and mechanical behavior.
